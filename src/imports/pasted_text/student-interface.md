# IMPORTANT DESIGN DIRECTION

Do not make these screens resemble a traditional LMS.

The student's main experience should feel like a **personal speaking development journey**.

Prioritize:

🎤 Speaking

📊 Assessment

🗣️ Practice

💬 Feedback

📈 Improvement

The dashboard should immediately answer:

**“Where am I now, what should I practice, and how much have I improved?”**

Keep the interface friendly, encouraging, modern, and distinctly KezSpeak.
