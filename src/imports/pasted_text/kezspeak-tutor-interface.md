**Assess → Evaluate → Give Feedback → Monitor Improvement**

# 17. VISUAL DIRECTION

Keep the tutor interface professional but friendly.

Use:

* Kez Blue
* Kez Yellow
* Light blue backgrounds
* White cards
* Rounded corners
* Soft shadows
* Clean charts
* Friendly icons

Do not make the tutor dashboard feel like a traditional school LMS.

The most important information should be:

**Who needs evaluation?**

**How are learners performing?**

**How much have they improved?**

**What feedback should I provide?**

---

# IMPORTANT

The tutor is an **assessor and mentor**, not a course administrator.

Prioritize:

🎧 Listening to recordings

📊 Evaluating speaking

💬 Providing feedback

📈 Comparing pre/post performance

👥 Monitoring learners

🎯 Identifying areas for improvement

Create realistic sample data and polished high-fidelity UI throughout.
